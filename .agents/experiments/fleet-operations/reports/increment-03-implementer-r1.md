Selection evaluation reserves stationary Colonize strength with Move in the aggregate budget, including edit, cancel, replacement, paired Move+Colonize, partial force, and exact-threshold behavior. Ruleset validation checks Colonize role mappings and field-level issues without relying on action IDs or names.

Public API and worker flows cover saved turns, reload, concurrent reservations and closure, game-scoped ownership, natural RNG persistence, and atomic rollback at the real worker save seam. Frontend cards expose generated Colonize parameters; the selected system and open planet pane now read refreshed ownership by stable IDs. Authenticated browser and Storybook flows show pending journeys, success, prevention at 19, stationary budgets, and alternate Seed Outpost content. Systems and glossary pages describe live behavior and current costs.

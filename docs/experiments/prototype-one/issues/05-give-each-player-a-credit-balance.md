# Heirs of Sol Prototype 1 · 05/26 · Give each player a credit balance

Agent: Codex gpt-6.2-astra high

## Description

Credits fund a player’s decisions across the match. A player who earns credits at home will later be able to spend them at another colony or use them to send a ship on a trip. Introduce the personal balance now so a player can enter a new match and see the funds available for their opening moves. Income and paid orders will use this balance as those capabilities arrive.

## Acceptance criteria

- Starting a match grants each player a personal credit balance, including in solo play.
- The player can see their balance in the game. One balance belongs to the player across all their colonies.
- Keep the starting amount available for tuning.

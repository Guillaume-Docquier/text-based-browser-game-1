# Ideological Axes

## Status

Planned

- [ ] Ideological Axes

## Context

We want the player choices to feel meaningful, immersive and impact the game they're playing. Roguelikes and ARPGs do this via the variety of character builds you can aim for, mixed with some RNG. Some strategy games will do this via a Tech tree, others will promote asymmetric playstyles.

We'd like to avoid general all-around strategies that you can always play. We want players to make a plan and invest in it.

## Decision

We'll center the gameplay around Ideological Axes. These axes will be what defines the empire and the available actions. Most actions will shift the empire's ideology on one or multiple axes. The positioning on the axis will dictate the set of actions available to be played during the turn.

For example, there could be an axis from Industrial to Ecological, where Industrial empires have access to actions that can harvest resources more easily at the cost of planet pollution which has negative impacts, while the Ecological action set preserves the environment but only allows the exploitation of renewable resources.

Extreme ideologies could require a lot of investment, but offer very strong, and biased, actions. Being extreme would also mean that you'll lose access to actions on the other end of the spectrum, so your empire will be less flexible.

Because your stance on an axis dictates what you'll be able to do (and what you will NOT be able to do), players will have to carefully balance which actions they take to preserve their desired alignments. Planning an ideological shift will also be part of the strategy, where you might want to start with a set of ideologies and shift later on.

## Pros

- Can lead to very thematic builds
- The player is in full control of their strategy
- The player can plan their ideological path over the long term
- Extreme ideologies offer a real choice between staying flexible vs super specialized

## Cons

- If not balanced properly, there might be certain ideologies that are no-brainers during the early/mid/late game

# Competitive Collaboration

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Most games are competitive. Collaborative playstyles are not very widespread. However, I think coop games are fun and open up dynamics that are not often explored.

## Decision

The game will incentivize players to collaborate while retaining individual scoring. Collaborating with other players should be the most viable options at some points of every game.

## Pros

- Competitive collaboration could lead to original mechanics not often explored.

## Cons

- Competitive collaboration could prove to be a hard constraint to satisfy.
- It could be confusing to players how competition and collaboration can play hand in hand.

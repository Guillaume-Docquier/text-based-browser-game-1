# Thoughts dump

## Tech Tree

Science unlocks and improves buildings and ships.

Science can be patented and traded with other players

## Trade

Players can trade shares of their own empire, giving parts of their points to other players at the end of the game.

Credits can also be traded, and credit income can be traded too.

Technology (patents), Planet data, and Ships are also tradeable.

The trade board accepts bids to break ties. Other activities where ties can occur can also accept bids to break ties.

A trade can offer multiple things, but must request either Credits or Shares in exchange. A small number of global currencies should make trades more likely to be accepted.

Trades take at least 2 Turns (posting the offer, then accepting the offer), encouraging infrequent trades with a large impact.

## Messages

Players can send messages, but messages are only delivered at the start of the next turn.

Messages can be broadcasted or DM'ed

## Agreements / Contracts

Players could offer contracts giving benefits in exchange for credits or shares.

For example, a player could offer first pick upon resource pickups on their planets to a certain player. This player would be advantaged when competing with other logistics players.

Another example could be free passage, where maybe by default players cannot transit through other players planets and must be granted free passage through a contract. Might work better as a restriction, where the restriction can be lifted in exchange for credit or shares.

## Transport contribution

Should transportation be worth more the furthest the resource was extracted? Transporting something for 2 LY should be worth less than transporting across 50 LY. It can't be total transport distance because then you'll just zig zag to farm.
